광고 (Google AdMob)

앱에는 화면 아래 배너 광고와 보상형 광고가 있습니다. 보상형 광고는 사용자가 결과 화면에서 「이어하기」나 「×2」(이번 판 해금 수치 2배)를 직접 눌렀을 때만 나옵니다. 광고는 Google의 Google 모바일 광고 SDK(AdMob)가 보여 줍니다.

  • 광고를 보여 주고, 광고 사기를 막고, 광고 성과를 재기 위해 Google이 기기에서 정보를 수집할 수 있습니다: IP 주소(이를 통한 대략적인 위치), 기기·운영체제 정보, 광고 노출·클릭 같은 광고 상호작용, 앱 충돌·성능 같은 진단 정보.
  • 앱은 추적 허용 창(App Tracking Transparency)을 띄우지 않습니다. 그래서 Google은 광고 식별자(IDFA)를 받지 않습니다. 앱은 모든 광고를 맞춤이 아닌 광고로 요청하며, 다른 회사의 앱·웹사이트 정보와 연결한 맞춤 광고나 추적을 하지 않습니다.
  • 유럽경제지역·영국·스위스 등 법이 요구하는 지역에서는 첫 광고 전에 Google의 동의 창이 뜨고, 설정의 「광고 개인정보 설정」에서 언제든 선택을 바꿀 수 있습니다.
  • Google이 정보를 처리하는 방식은 Google 개인정보처리방침을 따릅니다: https://policies.google.com/privacy , 파트너 사이트·앱에서의 데이터 사용: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Information the developer collects

The developer does not collect personal information. There is no account or sign-in, and the app does not ask for your name, email or contacts. It does not request access to the microphone, camera, location, contacts or photos.

Information stored on your device

Game records (best score, total cubes broken, a run to resume) and settings (sound, music, vibration) are stored only in the app's storage on this device. They are not sent to the developer and are removed when you delete the app. Apple device backup and restore follow your device settings and Apple's policies.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The app shows a banner ad at the bottom of the screen and rewarded ads. A rewarded ad plays only when you tap "Continue" or "×2" (double this run toward unlocks) on the results screen yourself. Ads are served by Google's Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob).

  • To serve ads, prevent ad fraud and measure ad performance, Google may collect information from the device: IP address (and the approximate location derived from it), device and operating-system information, ad interactions such as views and taps, and diagnostics such as crashes and performance.
  • The app does not show the App Tracking Transparency prompt, so Google does not receive the advertising identifier (IDFA). The app requests non-personalized ads only, and no ads are personalized by linking data from other companies' apps or websites, and no tracking takes place.
  • In regions where the law requires it (European Economic Area, UK, Switzerland and others), Google's consent form appears before the first ad, and you can change your choice at any time under "Ad privacy settings" in Settings.
  • Google processes this information under its privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy , and how Google uses data from partner sites and apps: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Other network use and third parties

Apart from ads, the app does not connect to the internet and uses no analytics, payment or sign-in tools. Tapping "Privacy Policy" in Settings opens this document in Safari, where GitHub's privacy policy applies.

Children

The app is not directed at children, and the developer collects no information from anyone, including children.
